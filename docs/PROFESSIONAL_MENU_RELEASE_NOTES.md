# Professional Menu Release Notes

This release completes the agreed Professional Menu operational product while preserving the current CARDfy V2 account foundation and legacy QR route.

Key areas included: customer menu and checkout, server-authoritative pricing, unified orders, tables and Table QR sessions, POS, staff roles, delivery dispatch, OTP, tracking, printing queue/reprint, operational settings and analytics.

The new Professional Menu remains on `/menu/` and does not replace the existing root QR renderer until a separate cutover is approved after final demo acceptance.
