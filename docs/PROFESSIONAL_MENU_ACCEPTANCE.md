# Professional Menu Final Acceptance Coverage

Final user acceptance should cover one planned demo tenant end to end:

1. Owner profile, theme, operating mode, opening hours and order types.
2. Categories, products, variants, reusable add-ons, discounts and coupons.
3. Public menu browsing, customization, cart and server-authoritative checkout.
4. Takeaway order lifecycle and tracking.
5. Delivery zone, assignment, driver PIN flow, OTP and customer feedback.
6. Dine-in POS table selection.
7. Table QR session, multiple orders, running total, bill request, payment and close table.
8. Owner/Coordinator orders view and status transitions.
9. Cashier/Coordinator/Delivery role restrictions.
10. Kitchen/cashier printing, failed-print warning and reprint.
11. Analytics for revenue, order types, sources, products and operational timing.
12. Mobile/desktop RTL smoke test and legacy QR regression.

The legacy root QR route must remain unchanged until an explicit cutover decision is made.
